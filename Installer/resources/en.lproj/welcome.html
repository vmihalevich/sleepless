With one click, Sleepless keeps your MacBook working even with the lid closed: the screen goes dark, while music, downloads, background tasks and remote connections carry on as usual!

This installer puts Sleepless into Applications and lets it run just one system command as administrator — the one that controls your MacBook’s sleep settings.

Your usual sleep settings come back as soon as you turn the switch off, quit or close the app, restart your Mac, or the battery drops to 10% or below.

Enjoy!
